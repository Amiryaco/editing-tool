# Copy and structure

## Voice

- Write as the business owner talking to another business owner, in plural address (״אתם״, ״השאירו פרטים״). It is warm, direct and confident, with no hype words (״מהפכני״, ״הכי טוב בישראל״) and no AI clichés (״בעולם של היום״, ״לא רק... אלא גם...״ chains, triplets of adjectives).
- Use short sentences, with one idea per sentence. Hebrew headlines work best as two or three short lines, the second line in gold carrying the payoff:
  - ״לא מבטיחים. / מראים מספרים.״
  - ״קמפיינר מנהל מודעות. / אסטרטג שיווק בונה מערכת.״
  - ״אל תאמינו לנו. / תשמעו אותם.״
- Use correct Hebrew punctuation. Don't put a comma before ״ו״ (״שיווקתי עסקים... וגם הקמתי״, not ״..., וגם״), but do use a comma where the reader pauses (״ואחרי כמה חודשים, מחפשים איש שיווק חדש.״). Use Hebrew quotation marks ״ ״ and geresh ׳ (לקוח א׳).
- Match the user's exact wording when they dictate a line. Fix grammar and say so in one line, but don't "improve" their message.
- Prefer the user's own phrasing for their story (the About section). Edit it for grammar and rhythm only.

## Hard rules

1. **No invented numbers, quotes or results.** Every figure comes from the user or their screenshots, transcribed 1:1.
   - When a figure is derived (estimated revenue = spend × the user's stated average ROI), label it on screen: ״הכנסות משוערות... לפי החזר ממוצע של פי 5״.
   - Back it up in the terms: ״אינו התחייבות״.
2. **Frame proof as a sample:** ״5 תוצאות מדגם מתוך חשבונות הלקוחות שאנחנו מנהלים״. Otherwise visitors think those are the only clients.
3. **Show money at first glance.** People want to see "money that came in". Put the key money figure on the card, not hidden in a breakdown.
4. **One combined figure per card.** If one account reports leads and another reports "פניות", show one number each, the same way across all cards.
5. **Name every platform the business works on, everywhere.** Here that meant Google, Meta and TikTok: the hero subhead, the FAQ, the method and the quiz. Missing one ("we also do Google") was an explicit correction.
6. **Big round claims stay words if there's no exact number:** ״מיליונים״, not ״₪מיליונים״ or a made-up ₪ figure.

## Hero

- **Headline:** the reframe that the whole page proves. It names who the visitor needs, not what you do. Example: ״העסק שלכם לא צריך עוד קמפיינר. / אתם צריכים אסטרטג שיווקי שמבין את כל חלקי הפאזל.״
- **Subhead:** one sentence listing the concrete pieces, ending in the outcome (leads, meetings, deals).
- **CTAs:** a gold primary that names the free thing (״לשיחת אסטרטגיה ללא עלות״) and WhatsApp as the secondary. On phones they go in one row.

## Story chapters (headlines only)

problem → turning point → result.
- **Problem:** ״מעלים תקציב. מחליפים מודעה. / מנסים קמפיין אחר. / ואחרי כמה חודשים, מחפשים איש שיווק חדש.״
- **Turning point:** ״שיווק שעובד / לא מתחיל בכפתור / ״פרסם״״
- **Result:** ״מערכת שיווק אחת. / שמכניסה עסקאות.״, then three facts (״פי 5 / 6 / מיליונים״ with a one-line caption each) and the CTA ״בואו נבדוק איפה השיווק שלכם נתקע״.

## Sections after the story

| Section | Job | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Results | Proof you can inspect | Eyebrow ״ישר ממנהל המודעות של מטא״. Show the summary row (ROI ×5, total leads, lowest cost per lead), then tabs per account: the image first, then the key figure, then a "full breakdown" button. See proof-sections.md. |
| WhatsApp wall | Social proof in the client's own words | Real screenshots, names blurred. Add a caption under each with the gist in text (accessibility). |
| Video testimonials | Faces and voices | 5–7 clips, Hebrew captions available, no autoplay. |
| Method | Why you, not "another campaigner" | Two columns, ״עוד קמפיינר״ vs ״אסטרטג שיווק״, with 4–5 contrasting lines each. |
| Quiz | Value before the ask | See quiz.md. |
| The call | Remove fear of the call | ״שיחה אחת. שלושה דברים שתצאו איתם.״ followed by 3 numbered steps (review, diagnose, exact proposal). |
| About | Trust in the person | First person, with a real photo (a sharp one; the user noticed a pixelated thumbnail). |
| Fit | Qualify and flatter | ״זה לא מתאים לכל אחד. / זה מתאים לכם אם:״ followed by 4 lines. |
| FAQ | Answer objections | The price of the call (free), how you're different, platforms, the price of the service ("depends, you get an exact quote"), and who actually runs the campaigns. |
| Form | The ask | ״מרגישים שהפרסום שלכם יכול לייצר הרבה יותר?״ + the response time (״נחזור אליכם תוך 24 שעות״) + who calls. Fields: name, phone, business (optional). Offer WhatsApp as an alternative. |

## Labels and small text

- **Eyebrows** (״ההבדל״, ״שיחת אסטרטגיה ללא עלות״): use a different, quieter font (IBM Plex Sans Hebrew, medium, slight letter-spacing) with a gold line on each side. The user felt the display font there "looked AI".
- **Button copy** names the outcome or the free thing, never ״שלח״ or ״לחץ כאן״.
